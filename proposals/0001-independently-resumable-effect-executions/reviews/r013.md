## Dedicated simplification

No material finding. No strictly smaller surface preserved all fixed guarantees:

- `NonParking` is independent of ordinary `fn` and `Detached` and is required to admit generic
  runtime-invoked callbacks safely.
- Exact executable plus sealed-property conjunction carries both representation identity and
  declaration-time proof without a runtime witness or a broader callable feature.
- Collapsing `O + R(&O)` into a reusable capturing callback either makes an affine owner capture
  consuming or retains a disallowed external loan.
- Removing branch state `D` requires cloning, shared allocation, or a new compiler-owned step sum.
- Notifying and DestroyPending are necessary semantic distinctions even when a backend fuses their
  representation.
- Splitting the combined Allocation adds a second procurement and reclamation path, losing the
  selected one-package admission guarantee.

Eager start, dormant sweeping, and runtime-funded fatal packaging remain smaller only by dropping
owner-selected first activation, task-specific push readiness, or recoverable caller-funded
admission respectively.
