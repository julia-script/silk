## Contract

The frozen thesis, claims C1–C8, and out-of-scope list are in [r001](r001.md). Revision 32 changes
the following claim details in response to Round 1:

- **C1:** only private zero-parameter Effect tests are eligible. Test compilation uses explicit
  user manifest test roots (falling back to `package.root`) or the standard-library test catalog,
  then unions and de-duplicates their transitive closures.
- **C2:** ordinary `silk.test` supplies its Reporter service identity as a type witness to the
  sealed inventory/invocation operation; the compiler validates marked residual requirements
  against that witness without recognizing a library actor by spelling.
- **C3:** `Test.run` uses the caller's lexical Reporter provision. Fresh reporter state and Effect
  scope are standard-runner policy; custom runners may share reporter state.
- **C4:** Event and Outcome use existing structs and structural unions, not enum declarations.
- **C5:** the selected runner remains a zero-parameter entry and reads raw filter arguments from
  the existing HostInput service; no test-specific entry adapter remains.

All other in-scope and out-of-scope claims remain as frozen in r001.

## Blockers

- S7 — assertion-level Reporter coupling is unearned in the minimal model.
- S8 — a call-site service witness is not a unique authority for eligibility.
- S9 — the runner executable root is missing from the compilation composition rule.
- E9 — test-root containment and canonical identity are undefined.
- S11 — the HostInput runner example violates current entry and service-closure rules.

## Revision response

- S7: moved structured reporting from assertions to ordinary runner consumption of a closed
  Outcome. Tests and Test.run no longer require Reporter; the replaceable service remains the
  presentation seam.
- S8: removed the call-site witness model entirely. Eligible tests are closed, and no compiler
  operation selects or compares a library service identity.
- S9: distinguished the selected runner executable root from inventory roots and defined their
  closure composition, entry discovery, de-duplication, and inventory exclusion rule.
- E9: required project test roots to resolve inside the existing package source root and derive
  canonical identities from it; repaired the manifest example.
- S11: made the standard runner a closed ordinary `main`, with an ordinary edge helper that
  constructs/provides OsHostInput, Allocator, and output services and maps infrastructure failure
  to status 2.

Not stopped — verified blockers were revised in Candidate revision 33.
