## Purpose

A complete evidence ledger distinguishes core closure from remaining roadmap obligations.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Absence and responsibility audit

Core closure SHALL require completed replacements, deleted obsolete paths, consistent prescriptive/generated documentation and required target evidence for JUL-120 through JUL-136, including exercised integer variadics. Failed checks SHALL identify the replacement owner rather than being waived by the audit.

#### Scenario: Remaining compiler adapter

- **WHEN** a completed slice still emits its superseded adapter
- **THEN** the audit fails and records the owning ticket

### Requirement: Complete roadmap ledger

The ledger SHALL account individually for WS/SPEC obligations, JUL-137 through JUL-146, partial capability remainders and corrected outcomes from JUL-148/149/150. Pending or unadmitted features SHALL not be represented as delivered.

#### Scenario: Partial ABI delivery

- **WHEN** integer variadics are delivered
- **THEN** floating/pointer/aggregate tails remain explicit obligations with their admission evidence

### Requirement: Verification evidence

Claims SHALL link exact implementation/tool/supply revisions and cheapest adequate semantic, ABI, object or native evidence. Required lanes SHALL fail missing supplies/skips. Repository gates SHALL run in order, including release:candidate for changed package contents.

#### Scenario: Unavailable required runner

- **WHEN** a designated target lane cannot execute its required checks
- **THEN** closure remains incomplete and identifies the missing evidence
