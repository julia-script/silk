The lexer and stack VM found pressure around named numeric vocabularies and shared reads of growable
Copy collections. This witness repeats neither wall: identifiers remain ordinary values, the inbox
is deliberately fixed-capacity, and the retained affine payload is observed by borrow. It instead
exposed general executable-cleanup defects, repaired without recognizing `Shared`, Deferred, inbox,
producer, waiter, or callback spellings. As in those reports, visible linear work and explicit
allocation remain preferable to unmeasured compiler/runtime privilege.

The removed wall is local shared ownership only. Callbacks run synchronously in registration order
after the state borrow ends. This does not prove SLP-0001 execution transfer, parking, wake
selection/order, cancellation, fairness, or scheduler integration. Evidence therefore points next
to the separate SLP-0001 execution-transfer/parking work; this witness remains its shared-state
building block rather than becoming a runtime API.
